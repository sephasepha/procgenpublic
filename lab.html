Reading the numbers

Dead ends, junctions and straight are shares of all cells. River is the average length of a dead-end branch: high means long blind alleys. Tortuosity is the corner-to-corner path length divided by the straight-line grid distance. Longest path is the maze's diameter as a share of cells. Loops counts extra connections beyond a perfect maze, which come from braiding.
