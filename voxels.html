How this works

The three structures share one cell grid. Each cell is a 3×3 room interior with a wall line, one storey tall. Cells are grouped into halls (single cells), rooms (open rectangles on one storey) and shafts (climbable columns). A spanning tree over those regions makes every one reachable, and braiding then gives dead ends a second way out. Storeys connect by 3-step stairs (with a hole above) or by shafts.

The Tower has an entrance on the ground floor and its goal on the top floor. The Pyramid narrows from a triangular base to a single top cell. The Sunken column starts at a stair in the surface and descends to its goal at the bottom.

A walk check then moves a two-voxel-tall explorer through the voxels: walking, stepping up or down one voxel, dropping and climbing shafts. It confirms every cell can be reached and left again. The red line is its route from outside to the goal.

For WFC, each voxel has a label (wall, floor, stair, door, shaft…), and each cell records its region, its kind and a socket on each of its six faces (wall, open, arch, door, entrance, stair, shaft, floor, roof). Markers (◦) are air with a meaning: doorways, shaft space, the entrance, the goal.

One finger drags to orbit, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan. Settings live in the URL, so a structure can be bookmarked.
